Algorithm

  1. Compute total = bpm * 60 * 24 * 365.2425 * lifespan.
  2. Compute baseSpent = bpm * 60 * 24 * 365.2425 * min(age, lifespan).
  3. On an interval, add elapsedBeats = elapsedSeconds * bpm / 60 to spent beats and subtract it from remaining beats.
  4. Clamp spent to total and remaining to 0.

UI layout

  1. Bank: show remaining beats as the primary number, with spent and remaining totals underneath.
  2. Progress rule: show spent percentage as a thin filled rule with labels at birth, now, and selected lifespan.
  3. Controls: add age to the existing pulse and lifespan controls.
  4. Scale: add a fixed 60 bpm * 60 years line for the once-per-second intuition.
