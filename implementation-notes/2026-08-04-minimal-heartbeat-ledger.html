Algorithm

  1. Compute beatsPerYear = bpm * 60 * 24 * 365.2425.
  2. Compute total = beatsPerYear * lifespan, spent = min(age, lifespan) * beatsPerYear + elapsedSeconds * bpm / 60, and left = max(0, total - spent).
  3. Render 100 calendar cells where cells below age are spent, cells below lifespan are left, and later cells are muted.

UI layout

  1. Bank: show remaining beats, spent beats, total beats, and a single progress rule.
  2. Calendar: show a 10x10 life grid with decade labels and a small legend.
  3. Controls: keep only age, lifespan, and bpm.
  4. Footnote: keep one source line and delete the explainer sections.
